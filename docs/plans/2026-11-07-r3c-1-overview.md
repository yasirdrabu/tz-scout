**Goal:** The Overview screen (§9) shows where every account is this quarter: KPI tiles, a pipeline funnel with targets and pace, exits, a scorecard by seller, offer and practice, and a detail drawer behind every number.

**Architecture:** Analytics computes everything at read time, the way the funnel does today. There are three endpoints: `GET /analytics/overview`, `GET /analytics/scorecard?tab=` and `GET /analytics/metrics/{key}`. The web screen replaces the placeholder at `/t/:tenant/overview`.

## Rulings (controller, 2026-11-07; owner took the map defaults)

1. **Quarter.** The calendar quarter in the tenant timezone:
   - `from` = local midnight on the first day of the quarter; `to` = now.
   - Week: `w = floor(days since from / 7) + 1`, and `n = ceil(days in quarter / 7)`.
   - Pace share = elapsed ms / quarter ms.
   - "Last quarter at this point" = the previous quarter's `from` to `from` plus the same elapsed ms.
2. **Entered this quarter.** An account's entry into flow stage k is the earliest entry into stage k or any later flow stage, so a skipped stage counts as passed at the later time. For the first stage, `arrivedAt` also counts. A band's count is the accounts whose entry falls in the window.
   - Exits: accounts whose current `exitStage` is that exit and whose `exitAt` is in the window.
   - Only live stages count.
3. **Opportunity and Won.**
   - Won is the flow stage with `locked === 'won'`.
   - Opportunity is the flow stage with trigger `deal_created`; with none, it's the flow stage just before Won.
4. **Money (owner default 1).**
   - An account's open value is the sum of its open deals' `amountCents`, each falling back to the offer's `priceMinCents` when it has no amount.
   - An account with no deal at all also takes the offer price.
   - Won value is the sum of the account's won deals' amounts (the same fallback) for deals with `closedAt` in the window.
   - With no offer, or no price (custom), the value is 0.
   - Pipeline accounts are those whose furthest flow stage is at or past Opportunity and before Won, with no exit, counted at the time of the read.
5. **Pace (owner default 2).** `expected = round(target × share)`. The band is:
   - "Behind pace" when `count < target × share × 0.9`;
   - "Ahead" when `count ≥ target × share × 1.05`;
   - "On pace" otherwise.
   - With no target there is no pace.
6. **KPIs.**
   - Sent to Won = won ÷ arrived, over accounts that arrived this quarter, as a percentage to 1 decimal, or null when none arrived.
   - Days to win = the median of whole days from `arrivedAt` to the Won entry, for accounts that entered Won this quarter, or null when there are none ("None yet").
7. **Seller of an account.** The seller of the latest assignment, by `assignedAt`, that has `acceptedHow` set. Accounts never accepted have no seller.
8. **By seller row (owner default 3: no target).** Rows are sellers with an assignment whose `assignedAt` is this quarter, on a visible account.
   - Assigned = distinct accounts assigned this quarter.
   - Accepted = those with `acceptedHow` set.
   - Time to accept = mean clock hours from `assignedAt` to `respondedAt`, rounded to whole hours. This is clock hours, not working hours, and the cost if wrong is copy only.
   - Steps on time = seller-owned plan steps (`ownerUserId` = seller) with `dueDate` in the quarter up to today, done with `doneAt`'s local day ≤ `dueDate`, divided by those steps.
   - Meetings = crm `meeting` activities this quarter on the seller's accounts (Ruling 7).
   - Opportunities and Won = the seller's accounts that entered those stages this quarter.
   - Pipeline = open value of the seller's pipeline accounts.
   - Win rate = won ÷ opportunities.
9. **Colour vs team (owner default 3).** This applies to Accepted, Time to accept, Steps on time and Win rate. The team value is the pooled ratio over the rows shown.
   - `good` when the row is at or better than the team (lower is better for Time to accept).
   - `attention` when it is worse by more than 10% of the team value.
   - Else `neutral`.
   - No colour (`null`) when fewer than 2 rows have a value.
10. **By offer and By practice.**
    - Matched = accounts with that offer, or practice, that arrived this quarter.
    - Opportunities, Won and Won value = those that entered the stage this quarter.
    - Win rate = won ÷ matched.
    - Pipeline = open value now.
    - Offer rows are offers that aren't archived, plus any archived offer with a number. Practice rows list every practice that isn't archived. "Led by {lead}" uses the lead's name, with no sub when there is no lead.
    - Win rate colour: `good` from 15%, `attention` under 10%, else `neutral`, and null when matched is 0. These are the mockup's bands.
11. **Visibility (owner default 4).** Every set of accounts is narrowed by `filterVisibleAccountIds`, so a seller under `own_accounts` sees only their own accounts. The team colour then turns off (Ruling 9).
12. **Drawer accounts.** The 6 newest by the time behind the number (entry, deal close, meeting, assignment or due date). The meta line is "{offer} · {source}", plus " · {seller}" when the account has one.
13. **Breakdowns.**
    - Stage, exit and KPI metrics show By source and By offer, and also By seller for stages at or past the assigned stage and for exits.
    - Seller cells show By offer and By source.
    - Offer and practice cells show By source and By seller, except practice cells, which show By offer instead of By source.
    - Bars count accounts, or sum cents for money metrics. A source is its top-level ancestor, as in the funnel.
14. **Not now:**
    - CSV export.
    - Saved sort.
    - Sorting by money is client-side over the returned rows.
    - Meetings pace and per-seller targets are dropped.
